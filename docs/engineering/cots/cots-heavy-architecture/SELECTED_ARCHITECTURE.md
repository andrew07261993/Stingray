# Selected architecture — Architecture C revised pressure source

## Status

The historical COTS-heavy trade selection remains **Architecture C: distributed cartridge mechanical bus**. Its three × 70 g embodiment is superseded for continued development.

Formal disposition: **ARCHITECTURE C PRESSURE REVISION READY FOR OWNER REQUIREMENT CLOSURE**.

CAD implementation is not authorized.

## Bounded selected source

`5–7 × [Halkey-Roberts V95000 Hydro 1F, 1/2-20 + Leland 86202Z 38 g + exact holder]`

`→ individual HP check valves → HP bus → regulator → fixed restrictor → buoy-adjacent differential relief → buoy`

Compatibility is **PUBLISHED CONFIGURATION SUPPORTED**. Five through seven is the useful range generated by the existing gas model; the final integer count waits for the owner mission values.

## Gas envelope

Four supported branches (152 g) fail warm qualification at 0 m. Seven (266 g) reach maximum passing qualification grid points of 7.5 m warm, 3 m nominal and 0 m cold. The complete 1–7 count matrix for eight cartridge families is controlled in `CARTRIDGE_COUNT_MATRIX.csv`.

If the mission exceeds the seven-branch envelope, a fewer-branch 60–150 g automatic/supplemental arrangement may be selected only after written application approval. No large pair currently exceeds **APPLICATION APPROVAL REQUIRED**.

## Mandatory pressure controls

- Independently checked source branches.
- HP allowable at specified temperature above maximum credible cartridge pressure with required margin.
- `≥3,000 psig` retained only as a screening floor.
- Qualified regulator between cartridge circuit and buoy circuit.
- Fixed restriction to bound transient/fault flow.
- Resettable buoy-adjacent differential relief where supplier evidence permits.
- No numeric relief setting until buoy operating differential and MAWP close.

## Field reset

The architecture remains field-resettable after a damage-free activation: isolate/depressurize, replace cartridges and mandatory bobbins/pins/seals/clips, inspect holders/checks, dry/inspect/repack buoy, reset mechanism, perform qualified leak/function checks and restore transport-safe configuration. Supplier rearm instructions and exact holder/engagement controls are PO deliverables. Routine depot rebuilding is not required by the selected concept.
