# Final field-reset architecture

## Verdict

**FIELD-RESETTABLE BY TRAINED FIELD PERSONNEL USING COMPLETE CERTIFIED CYLINDER-MODULE EXCHANGE.** Normal reset requires no fabrication, pressure-vessel opening or field recharge. The fired module is quarantined for qualified service-station recharge/refurbishment; an accepted spare module restores the article immediately.

## Recovered-to-ready sequence

| Step | Action and control | Tool/consumable | Acceptance / reject | Service class |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Apply transport inhibit, isolate recovery area and restrain article | released inhibit/fixture | no uncontrolled movement; reject damaged restraint | FIELD SERVICEABLE |
| 2 | Verify B10 has vented differential pressure and confirm zero residual by manufacturer procedure; do not loosen a pressurized connection | Leafield indicator/procedure | zero-energy state confirmed; otherwise quarantine | FIELD SERVICEABLE |
| 3 | Remove complete fired cylinder/valve/head/servo module using released retention sequence; fit recoil/transit protection | standard hand tools; Leafield caps | no damage/corrosion/icing deformation | OPERATOR FIELD REPLACEABLE after training |
| 4 | Quarantine fired module for qualified penetrator/diaphragm/O-ring inspection and certified recharge | tagged container | no field opening or recharge | DEPOT ONLY |
| 5 | Inspect water actuator, servo, hose, GIV and B10; replace manufacturer-defined water element and single-use seals | configured rearm kit | correct MPN/lot/life; clean, dry, undamaged | FIELD SERVICEABLE |
| 6 | Deflate by released LP path; rinse after salt exposure, dry and inspect fabric, seams, GIV/B10 areas and attachment | fresh water, drying kit, magnifier | no cut, abrasion, delamination, seam lift, corrosion or trapped water | FIELD INSPECTABLE |
| 7 | Install accepted charged spare; verify exact MPN, 342 g fill record, full gross mass, serial/lot and CoC status | 0.1 g scale; released control sheet | identity/mass match; reject underweight, expired, damaged or uncertified module | OPERATOR FIELD REPLACEABLE after training |
| 8 | Connect rated hose and actuator/servo interfaces to drawing-controlled configuration | manufacturer tools/consumables | correct witness/alignment; no unsupported adapter | FIELD SERVICEABLE |
| 9 | Reset arm/crosshead/locks/springs and inspect HBD damper per mechanical procedure | released reset fixture | full stowed engagement and transport-safe indication | FIELD RESETTABLE |
| 10 | Fold/repack dry softgood without obstructing GIV, B10 or relief discharge | folding template and pack consumables | extraction clear; no valve/line pinch | FIELD RESETTABLE |
| 11 | Perform released low-energy leak/functional checks without firing live cylinder; verify inhibit and record | approved check and checklist | no leak/incorrect indication; configuration complete | FIELD SERVICEABLE |

## Component classification

- Charged complete cylinder module: **OPERATOR FIELD REPLACEABLE** after training and with accepted spare.
- Water-sensitive trigger and manufacturer rearm parts: **FIELD SERVICEABLE**.
- Hose, GIV, B10, softgood and restraint: **FIELD INSPECTABLE; REPLACE ON FAILURE**.
- Fired cylinder-valve/head internal refurbishment and recharge: **DEPOT ONLY**.
- Custom pressure-vessel maintenance: not applicable; zero custom pressure vessels.

The exchange concept meets the hard field-reset requirement. Carry at least one certified spare charged module per deployed article when immediate restoration is required.
