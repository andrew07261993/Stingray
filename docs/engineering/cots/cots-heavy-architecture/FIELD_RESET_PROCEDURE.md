# Field reset procedure

Status: **CONDITIONALLY FIELD RESETTABLE — MODULE APPROVAL AND TWO DEMONSTRATIONS REQUIRED**

This is a service architecture and inspection sequence, not authorization for unprotected live-pressure work. Only trained field personnel using the released service manual, manufacturer tooling and rated fixtures may perform pressure tasks. Structural, buoy-seam, regulator-internal, puncture-head-internal or pressure-boundary damage goes to depot/manufacturer service.

## Recovered after deployment → ready to throw again

1. **Quarantine and make transport-safe.** Establish a controlled service area, fit the physical transport inhibit, tag the unit `DEPLOYED / NOT ARMED`, and restrain the mechanism. Reject any uncontrolled spring/arm state.
2. **Isolate gas and verify zero energy.** Read the protected pressure indication; use only the designed vent/isolation feature under the approved service procedure. Verify zero indicated pressure and no flow before opening a pressure compartment. Never infer safe state from a spent-cartridge appearance.
3. **Record as-recovered condition.** Photograph witnesses, cartridge indicators, water trigger, buoy, lines, arms, locks, crosshead, springs and damper. Record serial/lot/cycle and anomalies.
4. **Remove consumed cartridge modules.** Remove each cartridge/puncture module with the exact manufacturer tool/support method. Segregate punctured cartridges. Reject a cartridge with uncertain state, corrosion, damaged thread/face or missing lot/minimum-gross-weight mark.
5. **Inspect puncture heads and branch modules.** Inspect pin/seat/gasket/thread/support and branch check exterior. Exchange the complete line-replaceable module if pin damage, seal cut, corrosion, contamination, loose retention or reverse leakage is found; no field reshaping or improvised seal.
6. **Replace water-sensitive consumable.** Install the exact in-date water element/indicator/service kit for the approved inflator. Record lot and expiry. Reject wet, expired, damaged or configuration-mismatched parts.
7. **Deflate and drain buoy.** Use the supplier deflation feature, drain trapped water, and prevent relief-valve plugging. Do not fold under vacuum or apply unapproved suction.
8. **Wash/dry.** Fresh-water rinse where the environmental procedure requires it; dry the buoy, pack cavity, trigger cavity, lines and mechanism completely. Any unresolved moisture at the trigger is a reject.
9. **Inspect buoy and recovery attachment.** Examine fabric/coating, seams, inlet, relief, tube, harness, tether and terminations. Leak/pressure-decay test only at supplier-approved low differential. Seam/inlet/relief/harness damage is depot/manufacturer service, not a field patch for mission use.
10. **Inspect pressure lines and seals.** Check tube routing, abrasion, kinks, corrosion, fitting witness marks and seals. Replace single-use or time-limited seals with exact controlled parts. Pressure-boundary damage or an unverified fitting installation is a reject.
11. **Inspect damper and moving mechanism.** Check damper leakage, rod damage/corrosion, setting witness and smooth full stroke in the guarded low-energy fixture. Check crosshead, guides, arms, pivots, stops, deployed locks and stowed latches. Damper leakage, binding, cracked/deformed structure or missing positive lock requires replacement/depot disposition.
12. **Reset crosshead, arms and locks.** With the released reset tool, guarded fixture and defined reverse sequence, return the crosshead and arms, verify each deployed lock releases correctly, engage stowed retention and confirm captive hardware. Do not bypass spring forces by ad hoc leverage.
13. **Reset springs and ejector.** Recompress only through the designed reset interface, inspect for set/corrosion/damage, engage the latch and confirm preload/engagement witness. Gas-spring, backup-spring or ejector anomalies require component replacement.
14. **Reset/inspect damper.** Restore only the controlled setting and witness. Adjustment is a field-maintenance task, not operator-level; ACE requires external positive stops before stroke end.
15. **Fold and repack buoy.** Follow the approved fold card, orientation marks, pack gauge and routing that prevents cartridge/line/door interference. Record packed volume and closure witness. A snag, excess pack gauge or obstructed relief is a reject.
16. **Install new cartridge modules.** Verify exact released MPN, full/unpunctured face, minimum gross weight, lot/date and accepted receiving records. For the bounded revision, install each `86202Z` with its exact `V95000` service parts and controlled holder/engagement instructions. Larger cartridges remain prohibited unless their exact application is approved.
17. **Reset arm/release indicators and transport lock.** Confirm green/safe or equivalent approved indications, stowed locks, crosshead witness, closure witness and physical transport inhibit.
18. **Leak check.** Field-maintenance uses the approved rated low-energy inert-gas fixture and acceptance limit. Routine operator checks must not fire a CO2 cartridge. Any pressure decay beyond the released limit is a reject.
19. **Dry functional check.** Verify inhibit, manual witnesses, trigger-safe indication, arm captivity, relief unobstructed and sensor configuration without live discharge. Complete any released low-energy motion check in the guarded fixture.
20. **Final independent configuration verification.** A second qualified person checks part/lot/expiry records, cartridge count and mass, relief MPN/set marking, regulator/restrictor configuration, pressure indication, buoy fold, all locks/witnesses and transport-safe state. Sign `READY-TO-THROW` only after every gate passes.

## Field-reset verdict

The modular concept can avoid routine depot service after a successful, damage-free deployment. It is **credible but not yet demonstrated**. The exact `V95000 + 86202Z` rearm configuration closes the earlier cartridge/inflator service-kit deficiency. Remaining deficiencies are (1) no qualified submerged regulator/relief configuration, (2) no released holder/engagement values, leak limit or fold card, and (3) no two-cycle representative reset demonstration. Any candidate that requires puncture-head rebuilding, regulator overhaul, pressure-vessel fabrication or buoy-seam repair after normal use is unacceptable for Architecture C.
