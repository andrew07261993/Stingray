# Developmental versus release data gates

## A. Required before developmental CAD

Exactly one manufacturer-controlled document remains:

**LELAND DRAWING-CONTROLLED `89200` + `65026-18Y12` ARMED INTERFACE DEFINITION**

It must state the installed safe/armed and fired overall lengths, initial thread engagement, axial/rotary advance to full puncture, required puncture torque/force, cartridge retention/reaction requirement, bracket/outlet keep-out and removal direction. This is one interface document, not a request for multiple independent owner decisions.

No geometry shall be invented and no CAD shall start until this document is available.

## B. Required before procurement

- current orderable status and exact suffix for `89200`, `65026-18Y12`, `65027` and `65028`;
- manufacturer/distributor quote, lead time and minimum order;
- CoC option, country of origin and lot/marking traceability;
- exact commercial 1/8-NPT hose assemblies and pressure-control MPNs;
- exact water-sensitive consumable/trigger configuration and service kit;
- purchase-order quality clauses and no-substitution controls.

These are **PROCUREMENT / RECEIVING GATES**, not the single developmental-CAD blocker.

## C. Required before physical pressure test

- exact `89200` allowable working, proof and burst basis and temperature derating;
- exact `65026-18Y12` pressure-temperature rating and large-cylinder application limit;
- pressure rating/derating and cleaning/compatibility for each hose, fitting and pressure-control item;
- exact regulator/restriction flow data and safe blocked-outlet behavior;
- approved facility procedure, calibrated instrumentation, restraint, relief and exclusion controls.

The public DOT family test pressure and the `65026` 206 bar maximum inlet screen do not replace these values. No improvised pneumatic proof or burst test is authorized.

## D. Required before final release

- received-article identity and dimensional verification;
- accepted delivered-item manufacturer/distributor CoCs with lot/serial linkage;
- 0 C, 5 m-equivalent, dual-source useful-inflation test: at least 54 L actual displacement by 10 s and 60 L steady usable displacement;
- discharge synchronization, icing, residual mass, flow/restriction and leakage qualification;
- B10 relief-flow/accumulation, softgood pressure/leak/cycle and repeated salt-water reset qualification;
- corrosion, storage and marine environmental qualification;
- `V80040`-in-STINGRAY-latch immersion, splash/humidity, false-fire, timing, cycle and reset qualification; Nordson application approval is not claimed;
- final structural recovery/load-path evidence, including the preserved 500 lbf proof requirement and separate design/ultimate closure;
- owner/engineering release approvals.

Delivered-item CoCs accepted remain **0**.
