# Commercial CO2 cartridge-family trade — owner-case closure

## Decision

Leland `86202Z` (38 g, 1/2-20) remains the only retained exact Hydro 1F rearm pairing supported by captured published evidence. At the owner case, however, the first passing mass count is **nine**, not five through seven.

Nine `86202Z` cartridges total 342 g and clear the 304.10 g design and 334.51 g qualification requirements. This is not a released branch architecture because Nordson’s published V95000/manifold installation does not provide a documented HP outlet to the proposed check/bus.

Fewer 60–150 g cartridges remain packaging-attractive but **APPLICATION APPROVAL REQUIRED** with every retained water-automatic inflator. They cannot be promoted solely because their thread matches.

## Evidence limits

Exact cartridge gross/tare mass, production lot CoC, pressure-temperature declaration and controlled drawing remain RFQ items. Availability is a dated web observation, not a procurement record. Received identity and accepted delivered-item CoCs remain zero.
