# Type-3 development article

## Selection

**FUNCTIONAL-SCALE TEST ARTICLE — NOT REPRESENTATIVE OF FINAL GAS INVENTORY**

Quantity: **2 complete branches**.

Each article:

- Lifesaving Systems `481-CG` Pro Lite Vest, Auto with CG Markings, complete manufacturer assembly;
- installed Lifesaving Systems `470-CG` automatic inflator, published as Halkey-Roberts `V95000-1F`, including lanyard, gaskets and Super Bobbin;
- installed Lifesaving Systems `#484` CO2 cylinder, 33 g, 1/2-20, NSN `8120-01-646-0811`;
- manufacturer-provided bladder, inflator mount, cylinder support and protected low-pressure outlet path.

Compatibility classification: **PUBLISHED CONFIGURATION SUPPORTED**.

## Purpose and exclusions

The article proves V95000/V80040 water activation, puncture initiation, immersion behavior, rearm/reset and repeatability. It uses the same trigger/bobbin/puncture architecture as the preferred `V95000 + 86202Z` final-source baseline.

It does **not** demonstrate final 60 L gas inventory, final inflation time, final regulator/relief sizing, large-cartridge reaction loads or mission depth qualification.

## Test gate

The first live discharge uses the complete, unmodified `481-CG` system in a controlled tank/pool or qualified facility under manufacturer limits, with the vest softly restrained and personnel clear of the cylinder path. No improvised receiver, hose, fitting, holder, pressure tap or buoy connection is authorized. Instrumented pressure/flow testing is deferred unless LSC supplies an approved instrumented interface and a qualified pressure facility approves the procedure.

Minimum measurements: activation delay, successful puncture, water/cylinder temperature, video, cylinder mass before/after where the procedure permits, achieved vest inflation, visible icing/leakage, bobbin/pin/gasket condition and rearm time.

Development pass: both specimens activate and puncture on two controlled cycles without structural/seal failure; no detectable post-rearm leakage under the facility's approved low-energy leak check; rearm is completed using only published service parts/instructions. Activation delay is recorded, but the owner’s 54 L/10 s useful-inflation gate does not apply to this 33 g functional-scale article and cannot be inferred from it.

## Procurement hold point

Before PO release, LSC must confirm the quoted `481-CG` is supplied with current `470-CG`/HR `V95000-1F` and `#484`, plus replacement consumables, current instructions/revisions, lot/date traceability and CoC availability. The system is tested unmodified; no substitute inflator or cylinder is accepted.
