# Buoy pressure and relief gate

## HIKO result

HIKO's current `87640_OLV_ONE` page supports exact SKU, Drifter 210D TPU-coated nylon, abrasion resistance, cold flexibility and kayak-flotation use. It does **not** publish nominal/rated volume, operating differential pressure, MAWP, proof/burst pressure, inlet rating, relief setting/capacity/reseat, repeated inflation cycles or direct CO2 approval.

No numeric HIKO operating or relief pressure is authorized.

## Better-supported comparator

Subsalve enclosed flotation bags publish urethane-coated nylon construction, 2.5 psig (17.2 kPa) above-ambient relief, 6:1 safe-working ratio, integral hardware and 100% test before shipment. The smallest catalog `EFB-200` is about 100 kg/220 lbf lift and 5.4 kg packed; it is approximately 98 L, not a 60 L package, and its lift rating is below the owner-preserved 500 lbf proof-level recovery case. `EFB-500` clears 500 lbf lift but is roughly 245 L and 9.1 kg. Neither is a drop-in STINGRAY 60 L substitute.

The Subsalve pressure value is **TEST REFERENCE ONLY** and cannot be transferred to HIKO.

## Required low-pressure envelope

For the selected final buoy, the supplier must control:

- actual usable displaced volume ≥60 L at 5 m;
- operating differential range that produces stable geometry;
- MAWP at 0–32 °C water operation and applicable storage temperatures;
- relief crack/set tolerance, reseat and flow capacity with 5 m ambient backpressure;
- worst-case regulator-failure/restriction fault flow without exceeding MAWP;
- repeated immersion, saltwater, drying and reset/repack cycle limits;
- inlet/manifold and recovery-harness load separation.

Until those data exist, `P_operating`, `P_relief` and buoy MAWP remain **UNESTABLISHED — NO NUMERIC SETTING AUTHORIZED**.
