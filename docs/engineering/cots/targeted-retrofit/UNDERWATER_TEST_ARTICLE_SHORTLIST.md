# Underwater Test Article Shortlist

Maximum authorized recommendation: **3 item types x 2 specimens = 6 total articles**.

## Recommended purchases

1. **HIKO `87640_OLV_ONE` FLOATEK FULL TAIL — quantity 2.** Common to both architectures. This is the only retained exact 60 L manufacturer SKU and directly tests packability, ejection, inflation, seam integrity, leakage, tether load and field repack. The manufacturer page currently reports sold out, so procurement must confirm authorized dealer stock rather than substitute an adjacent size.
2. **Swagelok `316L-50DF4-150` — quantity 2, supplied empty.** Common to both architectures. This is the preferred qualified pressure-vessel lead. It tests exact package fit, serialized receiving evidence, external-pressure behavior, rated valve/relief integration and regulated gas delivery. Filling/charging is a separate approval and shall be performed only by a qualified pressure-system integrator.
3. **ACE Controls `HBD-15-25-AA-P` — quantity 2.** Likely COTS-heavy reuse. The baseline sealed damper has aluminum/steel/zinc-plated construction and no published underwater/external-pressure rating, making submerged force-speed drift, leakage and corrosion high-value physical unknowns.

## Category decision

- **Water activation hardware:** not purchased in this six-article lot. The existing exact V80040 bobbin has an official submerged-water IFU and is inexpensive to evaluate later in a lower-energy salinity/temperature coupon test. The complete Hydro 1F inflator remains deferred because its exact suffix and DF8 multi-cartridge/booster flow capacity are unverified.
- **CO2 inflation / valve / cartridge / reservoir:** selected through the empty Swagelok cylinder because pressure-boundary replacement has the highest safety and fabrication-reduction value.
- **Underwater mechanical actuator / damper / spring / release:** selected through the ACE HBD because external pressure and mixed-material corrosion are not addressed by dry CAD or the manufacturer data.

## Safety boundary

All high-energy pressure work must use qualified rated fixtures, correct gas-specific relief/protection, calibrated instrumentation, guarded/remote operation where appropriate, and manufacturer limits. No improvised pressure vessel and no uncontrolled pneumatic proof test are permitted. If manufacturer limits or the owner-approved mission pressure are unavailable, pressure testing does not proceed and the field remains **UNVERIFIED**.

Detailed adapters, concepts, instruments, data, provisional criteria, and chamber requirements are in `UNDERWATER_TEST_ARTICLE_SHORTLIST.csv`.
