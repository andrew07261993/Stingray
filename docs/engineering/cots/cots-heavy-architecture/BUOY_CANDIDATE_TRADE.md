# Buoy candidate trade

## Selection

Retain the **STINGRAY 60 L custom marine softgood** as the residual custom buoy, fitted with a commercial Leafield fixed-jet GIV and B10 Yellow differential relief. No standard pressure-characterized product close enough to 60 L also meets the existing mass and packaging basis. The retained softgood is not a custom pressure vessel.

## Evidence and exclusions

- Subsalve `EFB-200` provides 100 kg lift (about 97.6 L freshwater displacement), weighs 5.4 kg, packs to 0.38 x 0.28 x 0.18 m, and publishes urethane-coated nylon, RF-welded seams, a 3/4 NPT inlet, 2.5 psi-above-ambient relief, 100% factory testing and a 6:1 harness ratio. Its full-volume qualification inventory scales to about 544 g and it is too large/heavy. Source: [Subsalve enclosed flotation bags](https://subsalve.com/products/enclosed-flotation-bags/).
- Subsalve `SFB-100` derives only about 48.8 L from its 50 kg lift rating and fails volume.
- Subsalve `SFB-200` derives about 97.6 L and weighs 3.6 kg, but its package is 0.38 x 0.28 x 0.18 m and the product page does not publish relief setting. Source: [Subsalve cable and pipeline floats](https://subsalve.com/products/cable-and-pipeline-floats/).
- HIKO `87640_OLV_ONE` remains a folding/handling reference only. It has no published differential MAWP, relief, burst or cycle basis and is removed as default final buoy.

## Provisional pressure specification

- 60 L actual displacement at 5 m, not merely rated/geometric volume.
- Operating differential: 10.0 kPa (1.45 psi).
- Relief: Leafield B10 Yellow, 12.1 kPa (1.75 psi) nominal, 14.7 kPa (2.13 psi) maximum opening, 10.2 kPa (1.48 psi) minimum sealing.
- Softgood MAWP/repeated-cycle capability: **not released**; demonstrate leak/proof/cycle performance through the full relief-accumulation condition.

`PHYSICAL PRESSURE QUALIFICATION REQUIRED`

The provisional definition is adequate for bounded CAD interface work once the Leafield configured source drawing is obtained; it is not adequate for product release.

## Type-1 test article change

HIKO is removed from final-architecture qualification. Type 1 becomes **two identical 60 L STINGRAY engineering softgood articles fitted with the selected Leafield GIV and B10**, after bounded CAD/test-article definition. They are not authorized for purchase or fabrication by this commission.
