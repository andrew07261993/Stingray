# Six-article development RFQ package

## Scope and count

Maximum three types, two articles per type, six total:

1. Two HIKO `87640_OLV_ONE` 60 L buoy articles.
2. Two ACE `HBD-15-25-AA-P` dampers.
3. Two complete LSC `481-CG` automatic systems with installed `470-CG` + LSC `#484`.

Replacement cylinders, bobbins and gaskets are test consumables, not additional test-article types. No purchase was placed.

## Common quote requirements

Quote exact manufacturer and MPN, revision, seller status, quantity, unit/extended price, lead time, country of origin, manufacturer CoC option, distributor CoC option, lot/date/serial trace, supplied drawings/instructions and non-cancellable terms. State deviations explicitly; no equal/substitute is accepted without engineering review.

Keep these stages separate: **CATALOG IDENTITY VERIFIED**, **COC AVAILABILITY CONFIRMED**, **PROCUREMENT RECORD AVAILABLE**, **RECEIVED ARTICLE IDENTITY VERIFIED**, and **DELIVERED-ITEM COC ACCEPTED**. The last two remain false/zero.

## Type 1 RFQ — HIKO

- Exact MPN `87640_OLV_ONE`, quantity two identical, 60 L.
- Confirm retailer is authorized/traceable and permitted to export to the delivery location.
- Supply manufacturer label/lot/date images or records, construction/material specification, inspection/repair instructions and any operating-pressure/relief limitation.
- No substitution to a generic 60 L bag.
- Backup if the exact primary cannot be traced: HIKO `87901_ONE` Buoyancy RF V.2, quantity two, development-only after manufacturer identity/spec confirmation.

Current outcome: HIKO direct is sold out; KajakarShop.cz and other EU retailers displayed exact MPN inventory/add-to-cart. Dealer authorization and delivered traceability are not yet verified.

## Type 2 RFQ — ACE

- Exact `HBD-15-25-AA-P`, quantity two; 25 mm stroke, `AA` end fittings and `P` both-direction damping.
- Supply current drawing/data revision, force range, operating temperature, adjustment guidance, positive-stop requirement, recommended mounting and CoC option.
- Provide written guidance on temporary/continuous immersion and offshore/seawater exposure. “Sealed” is not accepted as seawater qualification.
- Quote serial/lot trace and any factory force/setting record.

Current outcome: exact suffix is orderable on request; observed distributor stock was zero and lead time is unverified.

## Type 3 RFQ — LSC/Nordson

For each of two articles:

- one complete LSC `481-CG` Pro Lite Vest, Auto with CG Markings;
- installed current LSC `470-CG` automatic inflator assembly, exact HR `V95000-1F`;
- installed LSC `#484`, 33 g, 1/2-20 cylinder;
- manufacturer bladder, inflator mounting and cylinder support, unmodified;
- current bobbin/gaskets/pin/clip and controlled rearm/owner instructions.

Quote replacement LSC `#474` Super Bobbins, `#479-A2` gasket sets and additional `#484` consumable cylinders separately. Confirm bobbin expiry, system/inflator/cylinder lot trace, CoC availability, cartridge gross-weight acceptance and application limits. The first tests use the complete unmodified system; engineering will not add an improvised pressure tap or outlet.

Label on PO and test record: **FUNCTIONAL-SCALE TEST ARTICLE — NOT REPRESENTATIVE OF FINAL GAS INVENTORY**.

## Receiving gate

Quarantine on receipt. Verify exact label/MPN/revision, quantity, dimensions where applicable, damage, date/lot/serial, bobbin expiry, cylinder gross weight against supplier limits, and document package. Engineering accepts each CoC against the PO before release to test. Delivered-item CoC count remains zero until then.
