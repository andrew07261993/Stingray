# Leland 89070 application review

## Disposition

Compatibility classification: **FIT APPEARS POSSIBLE — APPLICATION APPROVAL REQUIRED**

This classification applies to the proposed 70 g cartridge plus a water-activated inflator. It is not a production approval. No retained source states that a Halkey-Roberts V85000/V90000/V95000 or another water inflator is approved for Leland `89070`.

## Interface compatibility review

`1/2-20` establishes only nominal thread-family plausibility. The 205 mm long, 30 mm diameter cartridge is larger and has more reaction mass than common 24–38 g lifejacket rearming kits. Public Halkey-Roberts evidence says its threaded inflators are available in 1/2-inch variants; distributor application tables show up to 60 g in at least one V85000 use. No source found states 70 g capability. PFD service guidance also requires matching inflator model, thread and gas mass rather than thread alone.

The following remain application-approval gates:

- puncture-pin diameter, travel, tip form and puncture energy versus the 89070 cap;
- neck-face gasket compression, sealing land and thread engagement;
- cartridge axial/radial support and retention under discharge reaction/thrust;
- valve seat and outlet geometry after puncture;
- full-flow area, two-phase CO2 capacity, cold icing and blocked-flow behavior;
- permitted cartridge mass/length and manufacturer large-cartridge restrictions;
- inflator body load, attachment-to-buoy load and safe-state indication;
- exact water element, service life and replacement instructions.

The official Leland `65026-18N12` puncture device is 1/2-20, 1/8 NPT, stainless, CO2-compatible and typically stocked, but its public page does not state a permitted cartridge mass or water activation. It is therefore useful as an RFQ/reference candidate, not a verified Type 3 water branch. Source: [Leland 65026-18N12](https://www.lelandgas.com/product-page/copy-of-65026-18n12-puncture-device-in-line-non-mountable).

## Required vendor response

Obtain a written Leland and inflator-manufacturer application statement tied to `89070` and the exact inflator/puncture MPN. It must provide the interface drawing, allowed fill mass, retention/support requirement, pressure/temperature envelope, discharge orientation, flow/Cv or transient data, reaction load, icing restrictions, service parts, lifecycle and certificate options. Until then: **DO NOT PROCURE OR FIRE A COMPLETE 70 g WATER-ACTIVATED BRANCH**.
