# Inflator / cartridge compatibility closure

## Result

The exact preferred supported pair is:

`Nordson MEDICAL / Halkey-Roberts V95000 Hydro 1F, 1/2-20 configuration + Leland 86202Z 38 g`

Classification: **PUBLISHED CONFIGURATION SUPPORTED**.

The captured evidence is Leland's exact `V95000-86202Z` rearm kit plus Nordson's current Hydro 1F drawing, product description and service instructions. The exact lanyard suffix and handed cylinder-holder kit remain installation choices that must be frozen on the RFQ; they do not change the 1/2-20 inflator body family.

## Why this is not approval for 70 g

Nordson publishes 1/2-20 and 3/8-24 Hydro 1F variants, but does not publicly publish a universal cartridge-mass allowance. No official evidence captured here approves `89070`, its 8.07-in body, reaction load, support method or flow transient with Hydro 1F. The `V95000 + 89070` pair therefore remains **APPLICATION APPROVAL REQUIRED**.

The same restriction applies to 60, 86, 92 and 150 g automatic pairings. Leland `65026-18N12` is a commercial 1/2-20 manual puncture head, not a water-automatic inflator, and its public page does not close large-cylinder support limits.

## Published Hydro 1F architecture

- Automatic water activation using replaceable `V80040` bobbin.
- Manual pull-lanyard backup.
- 1/2-20 configuration when the drawing's 3/8 suffix is absent.
- Service items include bobbin, gasket and puncture pin; rearming must follow the exact manufacturer instructions.
- Nordson publishes UL 1191 and CEN/ISO 12402-7 context. This is component evidence, not STINGRAY system qualification.
- Nordson's universal-holder instructions identify `V90139UCHKIT` and `V90139UCH2KIT`; handedness and installed restraint must be selected from the controlled installation.

## Type-3 closure

Lifesaving Systems publishes `470-CG` as a complete replacement Halkey-Roberts `V95000-1F` inflator and pairs the relevant equipment with LSC `#484`, a 33 g, 1/2-20 CO2 cylinder. This gives a current commercial functional-scale path using the same V95000/V80040 trigger, puncture and rearm architecture as the preferred 38 g source.

Classification: **PUBLISHED CONFIGURATION SUPPORTED**.

It is not representative of final gas inventory and may not be connected to an improvised receiver. Live discharge requires a qualified, pressure-rated, restrained and remotely operated test fixture.
