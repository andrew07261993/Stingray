# Migration plan from corrected DF8

This is a future plan, not authorization. The corrected baseline stays immutable.

1. **Owner baseline gate:** visually inspect the exact final-cleanup AP242 masters in Creo; record acceptance and discrepancies.
2. **Architecture gate:** accept/reject Architecture C and close deployment depth, cold temperature, inflation time, buoy pressure and recovery load requirements.
3. **Vendor gate:** obtain exact inflator/cartridge configuration drawings, CAD, ratings, CoC options, lifecycle and RFQ evidence; freeze selected MPN suffixes.
4. **Source branch:** create a new CAD implementation branch/worktree directly from corrected DF8 commit `8c594781...`, never from Targeted-COTS.
5. **Interface preservation:** freeze external penetrator, normal-body, length, arm, transport and recovery datums; derive interface-control documents.
6. **Remove legacy pressure concept:** retire four-12 g carrier and custom booster vessels only after the new gas budget passes. Do not reuse baseline validation claims.
7. **Package modules:** lay out three protected 70 g branches, COTS checks/fittings/relief, pneumatic release, service indicators and keyed cartridge access; mounts should be non-pressure-bearing.
8. **Mechanism integration:** retain or redesign common crosshead, gas spring, damper, backup spring, stops and locks using exact vendor envelopes and source-derived interfaces.
9. **Softgoods/recovery:** select qualified buoy and structural harness/tether interfaces; ensure pressure lines and bladder film carry no recovery load.
10. **Service design:** model vent, cartridge keying, bobbin access, branch replacement, leak-test port, reset tool paths and repack fold card.
11. **Analysis:** transient two-phase gas model, structural loads, tolerance/envelope, mass/CG, corrosion/FMEA and service hazard review.
12. **Physical development:** execute the three common article types in rated facilities before committing detailed packaging.
13. **Full verification:** rebuild endpoint, connectivity, pressure, motion 0–80°, reverse reset, environmental, physical and release evidence against exact new masters.

No legacy AP242 parity, 0–55° motion checkpoint, endpoint validation, or final-cleanup targeted inspection is carried forward as proof of the changed architecture.
