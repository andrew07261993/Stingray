# Test fixture requirements

## Common controls

Every fixture requires an approved drawing/configuration, article restraint, calibrated instruments traceable to the test record, protected/remote operation where stored gas is present, defined personnel exclusion zones, controlled abort/safe-state handling and post-test inspection. Facility procedures and manufacturer limits control. No proof or burst test is authorized.

## Type 1 — HIKO 60 L softgoods

- Non-cutting pack-canister surrogate with adjustable but recorded confinement and a safe extraction path.
- Supplier-compatible low-energy inflation connection; no direct 70 g CO2 branch.
- Low-range differential-pressure transducer, temperature sensors, source-mass or flow measurement and synchronized video/time.
- Displaced-volume or buoyant-load measurement capable of resolving the 54–60 L screen.
- Low-differential pressure/volume hold measurement and a non-damaging attachment-load arrangement.
- Pool/open-water support for visible unfolding; rated hydrostatic chamber only for an approved depth case.

## Type 2 — ACE HBD damper

- Guarded linear actuator with load cell, displacement encoder, controlled velocity and specimen alignment.
- External positive stops 1–1.5 mm before both damper stroke ends as required by ACE.
- Submerged bath/fixture compatible with the defined water chemistry and temperature; collect any leakage.
- Representative non-corroding pins/end supports without imposing unintended side load.
- Chamber-rated feedthrough and fixture only if depth simulation is later authorized.

## Type 3 — blocked 70 g branch

No fixture is to be built around an unapproved inflator. After vendor approval, a qualified pressure facility shall specify a rated receiver or approved buoy interface, HP and LP calibrated transducers, fast temperature sensing, flow or cartridge mass measurement, relief capture/vent routing, article restraint, shielding/remote activation and a verified safe depressurization state. All hoses, fittings, sensors and adapters must be rated above the applicable node pressure. Hydrostatic chamber exposure and pneumatic discharge/proof are separate procedures.

## Instrument calibration floor

Select ranges only after the exact procedure is approved. As a minimum, resolution must distinguish the acceptance criterion: low-range buoy differential and leakage for Type 1; ±20% force-change screen and full 25 mm travel for Type 2; source/downstream transient, activation time, delivered mass and relief accumulation for the future Type 3.
