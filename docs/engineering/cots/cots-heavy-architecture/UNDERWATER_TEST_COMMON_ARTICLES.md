# Common underwater development articles

> Historical Phase 4 shortlist. The controlling immediate plan is now `UNDERWATER_TEST_PLAN.md`: HIKO ×2 and ACE HBD ×2 are recommended; the complete 70 g branch is blocked at quantity 0 until inflator approval.

Maximum retained: **three item types, quantity two per type**. These are development articles, not procurement release. High-energy pressure work must use appropriately rated systems, remote/protected operation, responsible pressure-system review, and facility procedures.

## Type 1 — water-triggered cartridge branch

- Candidate: Halkey-Roberts V95000 exact 1/2-20 suffix + V80040 + Leland 89070 70 g CO2; quantity 2.
- Test question: Does the complete water-sensing/piercing branch activate repeatably after representative saltwater, temperature and orientation conditioning without false fire, and can it be correctly serviced/reset?
- Fixture: vendor-compatible protected inflation/discharge fixture in a rated underwater pressure facility; no improvised vessel or exposed discharge.
- Instrumentation: water pressure/depth, temperature, activation time, cartridge mass before/after, upstream/downstream pressure and flow, high-speed event observation, leakage, service time.
- Pass/fail concept: both articles satisfy the owner-approved activation window, no false activation, safe containment, expected discharge mass, no blocked flow, correct safe/full indication, and two controlled reset cycles.
- Cross-architecture relevance: water authorization for A/C; sensor/pilot evidence for B; service maturity for all.

## Type 2 — 60 L-class buoy plus pressure-control train

- Candidate: exact qualified 60 L-class buoy MPN **UNVERIFIED — DESIGN GATE REQUIRED**; Swagelok SS-4C-1/3 and KVV11DE1 candidates; quantity 2 complete buoy/control articles.
- Test question: What gas mass, flow, transient pressure and relief behavior are required at specified depth and cold condition, including full-charge and one-source-out cases?
- Fixture: manufacturer/facility-rated underwater inflation rig with protected remote gas release, calibrated containment volume and safe relief capture.
- Instrumentation: depth/ambient pressure, gas and water temperature, cartridge/cylinder mass, inlet and bladder pressure, flow, buoy volume/shape, inflation time, relief flow, leakage and post-test inspection.
- Pass/fail concept: owner-specified usable buoyancy and inflation time achieved without exceeding supplier MAWP, uncontrolled relief, seam/inlet damage, or unacceptable residual leakage; partial-source criterion separately approved.
- Cross-architecture relevance: directly sizes A/C cartridges and B charge/valves; qualifies the common buoy, check and relief behavior.

## Type 3 — underwater release/damper/reset module

- Candidate: Clippard SRR-14-4 release cylinder + ACE HBD-15-25-AA-P + representative GN 817 plunger/lock; quantity 2 modules.
- Test question: Can the passive pressure release retract the lock and can the damper control motion after immersion/contamination, then reset repeatedly without binding or latent unsafe state?
- Fixture: guarded low-energy mechanism fixture reproducing release load, travel, side load, stop and lock interfaces; rated pressure supply and protected actuation.
- Instrumentation: input pressure, release force/travel/time, damper force-speed/displacement, lock engagement, reset force/time, leakage and temperature.
- Pass/fail concept: both modules release within approved pressure/time, complete commanded travel, engage positive lock, remain captive, show no seizure/leak, and complete the approved reset-cycle count.
- Cross-architecture relevance: common arm-release, damping, lock and reset behavior in A/B/C.

No purchase should exceed two specimens per type under this common-article recommendation without a separate test/procurement decision.
