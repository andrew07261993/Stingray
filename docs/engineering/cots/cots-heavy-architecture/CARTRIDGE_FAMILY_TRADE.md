# Commercial CO2 cartridge-family trade

## Decision

Leland `86202Z` (38 g, 1/2-20) is the preferred **published-configuration-supported** cartridge baseline because Leland publishes an exact `V95000-86202Z` Hydro 1F rearm kit. It is not automatically the final mission source: five to seven branches cover only a bounded shallow envelope, and the seven open owner requirements still control final count.

Fewer 60–150 g cartridges are packaging-attractive, but no captured official source approves those exact cartridges with a water-automatic inflator. They remain **APPLICATION APPROVAL REQUIRED** and cannot displace the 38 g baseline yet.

## Evidence and method

- Identity, charge, dimensions and thread were taken from current or legacy official [Leland cylinder listings](https://www.lelandltd.com/cylinders.html) and exact product pages. Published figures are reference data; purchasing requires the controlled drawing.
- Exact automatic pairings were accepted only when an exact Leland rearm kit or configuration was published. A `1/2-20` thread statement alone was not accepted.
- Gross mass was not published for the retained disposable cylinders and is **UNVERIFIED**. No tare-mass estimate is used as fact.
- Packaging volume in `CARTRIDGE_COUNT_MATRIX.csv` is an outer cylindrical-envelope proxy, not installed volume; brackets, holder clearance, hose bends and service access remain additional.
- `89150` has conflicting length information within current official material. It cannot enter layout until Leland supplies a controlled drawing.

## Practical branch-count result

Four through seven branches were explicitly evaluated. Four `86202Z` cartridges provide 152 g and fail warm qualification at 0 m. Five, six and seven provide 190, 228 and 266 g. At seven, the qualification grid limit is 7.5 m warm, 3 m nominal and 0 m cold. Missions beyond that require more than seven supported small branches or written approval of a larger-cartridge arrangement.

- Four branches are mechanically simpler but cannot be the supported 38 g solution.
- Five to seven branches are retained as the practical supported range. Reset burden becomes high at six/seven, but each branch remains independently serviceable and checked.
- More than seven small automatic branches is not retained because holder volume, trigger synchronization, connections and reset burden grow without closing the 15 m cold case.
- Fewer larger sources are thermodynamically superior: three `89150` cartridges (450 g) pass the 15 m warm/nominal and 10 m cold qualification grid; four (600 g) pass all grid points. Five `89092` cartridges (460 g) reach 15 m warm/nominal and 10 m cold; six pass all grid points. These results do not confer inflator approval. `89092` and `89150` remain **APPLICATION APPROVAL REQUIRED**.
- `85202Z` and `89150` have captured official dimensional conflicts. Their matrix envelope values are explicitly proxies until controlled drawings resolve them.

## Retained evidence limits

Availability is a web observation dated 2026-08-24, not a procurement record. CoC availability is not confirmed until stated on a quote/PO acknowledgment. Received identity and delivered-item CoC acceptance remain zero.
