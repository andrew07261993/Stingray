# Pressure-source packaging envelope

## Controlled boundaries

- Hard external diameter: **57.15 mm maximum**.
- Normal body target: 53.0 mm; no source fit credit is taken outside the hard maximum.
- Maximum rigid length: 2032 mm.
- Maximum ready-to-throw mass: 18.14 kg.
- Required mass reserve: at least 1.0 kg.
- Required qualification inventory: at least 334.51 g CO2.

## Practical source allocation

The architecture screen reserves 3.175 mm radially per side for body wall, support, anti-chafe, removal clearance, and tolerance. The resulting cylinder-body allocation is:

`57.15 mm - 6.35 mm = 50.80 mm`

Thus:

- cylinder body OD: **50.80 mm maximum**;
- preferred nominal OD: **50.0 mm or less** to recover tolerance;
- valve/head radial keep-out: **57.15 mm maximum**, independently demonstrated;
- axial service keep-out: cylinder length plus valve/head connection, recoil restraint, hose bend, and tool/extraction access;
- no hose, fitting, guard, or support may project beyond 57.15 mm in the stowed state.

The 1.250 mm body-wall and other radial allocations are provisional screening values. Bounded CAD must replace them with the actual wall/support stack without increasing the external envelope.

## Longitudinal screen

The controlled 2032 mm rigid length does not establish a free source bay. Existing documentation identifies a 227 mm commercial-cylinder concept as plausible but tight; it does not authorize a longer bay. Consequently:

- Leland `89200`, 234.95 mm cylinder body: near-bay candidate; requires at least about 250 mm clear source length before trigger/head/hose service keep-out;
- Leland `89150`, 187.96 mm cylinder body: body length candidate;
- Swagelok `316L-50DF4-500`, 597 mm: not accepted without a controlled longitudinal allocation and fails mass/interface independently.

No axial fit is claimed until a supported source/head assembly exists. This is not permission to redesign CAD.

## Mass screen

The prior Architecture C estimate was 14.0 kg using a 2.489 kg nine-source proxy. Therefore the fixed estimate excluding that proxy is 11.511 kg. To retain 1.0 kg reserve:

`maximum new complete pressure-system mass = 18.14 - 1.00 - 11.511 = 5.629 kg`

The best geometric dual Leland source remains well inside this mass screen. The 5000 psig Swagelok single-cylinder concept is estimated at 6.7 kg complete and fails it. Exact mass roll-up remains downstream of selecting a supported source/head configuration.

## Packaging gate result

The former 82.5 mm selection is rejected. A 50.8 mm body can be screened, but there is no published Leafield W28.8 cylinder at this diameter and no published Leafield 1/2-20 or 1/4 NPT source interface. Packaging alone therefore cannot authorize CAD.
