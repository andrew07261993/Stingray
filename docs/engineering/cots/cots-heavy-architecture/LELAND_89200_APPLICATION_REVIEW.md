# Leland 89200 application review

## Result

Two Leland `89200` cylinders close the gas-mass and cylinder-body diameter screens, and Leland's published `65026-18Y12` mountable puncture-device configuration provides a commercial high-pressure boundary with matching 1/2-20 and 1/8 NPT interfaces. The pair is classified:

**COMPATIBLE WITH PUBLISHED COMMERCIAL INTERFACE**

This does not mean Leland has published an armed, water-triggered `89200` application. The missing pre-CAD evidence is one drawing-controlled `89200` + `65026-18Y12` armed interface definition, including installed overall length and the advance/torque needed to go from transport-safe engagement to complete puncture.

## 65026-18Y12 review

- Function: fixed, mountable, twist-to-puncture commercial device; integrated stainless puncture pin, seal, filter and cartridge-disengagement relief.
- Published family interfaces: `65026-18Y12` is 1/2-20 inlet and 1/8 NPT outlet.
- Published maximum inlet: 206 bar / 3,000 psi.
- Construction: 416/proprietary stainless family body; current mountable series uses separately purchased stainless bracket `65027` and nut `65028`.
- Actuation: advancing/twisting the puncture-type cylinder onto the fixed pin; no water trigger, valve, regulator or flow shutoff is integrated.
- Reset: remove the discharged cylinder only after pressure is verified absent; inspect the pin/seal/relief and fit a certified charged replacement. Exact inspection/replacement interval is manufacturer-controlled.
- Published envelope proxy for the current mountable `65026` series: 1.775 in / 45.085 mm long and 1.23 in / 31.242 mm diameter. Exact `18Y12` installed envelope remains drawing-controlled.
- Orderability: exact legacy order code is published; current direct-stock status for `65026-18Y12`, bracket and nut is unverified.

The current `65026-18Y58` page confirms the mountable family remains commercially active, but it is a 5/8-18 variant and is not substituted for `18Y12`.

## Rejected shortcut

Leland `50043-003` is a current, typically stocked 1/2-20 puncture regulator with a 5 psi outlet and 1/8 NPT port. It is not selected: its public page relates it to small cartridges, and does not publish `89200` approval, inlet limit, large-cylinder reaction/support, or the flow needed for 60 L useful inflation in 10 seconds.
